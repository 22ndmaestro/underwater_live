👥 Crowd simulation

Temporary like, share & comment bubbles never exceed two-thirds the diameter of the second-largest gift bubble. The crowd generates new gifters, returning gifters, likes, shares & comments. Gift FX: 100+ coins = Pearl Burst, 1,000+ = Tidal Crown, 10,000+ = Leviathan.

How it works

Each viewer has one main bubble. For non-gifters, like streaks increase bubble size logarithmically & extend lifetime. Once a viewer gifts, their bubble becomes persistent & ONLY additional gifts can grow it. Likes, comments & shares create merging avatar animations without affecting the size or gift ranking.
